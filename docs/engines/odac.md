# ODAC Engine

<p class="metis-section-label">RegulatoryCommittee</p>

The ODAC engine studies FDA advisory committee cases: concern activation, panel
vote structure, briefing-document signals, and the bridge from advisory vote to
regulatory decision.

## What It Tries To Judge

- Which clinical or regulatory concerns become vote-relevant.
- Whether panel discussion changes the likely vote structure.
- How advisory committee signals translate, or fail to translate, into FDA
  action.
- Which replay lessons can become useful for future live cases.

## What It Does Not Try To Do

- Treat advisory votes as mechanical FDA decision predictors.
- Ignore briefing-document asymmetry.
- Collapse safety, efficacy, subgroup, and regulatory-process concerns into one
  generic risk bucket.
- Force live predictions when the case trigger is not ready.

## Current Public Lesson

ODAC is currently most valuable as a replay-rich engine. It can test whether
concern activation patterns transfer to other committee-like domains, including
FOMC vote-topology work, without pretending that regulatory committees and
central banks share the same decision mechanics.

## Recent Public Status

The public portfolio lists ODAC as monitoring. That means the engine remains
available for live or replay case triggers, but it is not currently the primary
weekly engine.
