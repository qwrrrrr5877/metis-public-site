# AI_CAP Engine

<p class="metis-section-label">SemiconductorCompute</p>

The AI_CAP engine studies AI infrastructure scarcity: advanced process capacity,
CoWoS packaging, HBM supply, hyperscaler capex, and the translation from supply
constraints into investment theses.

## What It Tries To Judge

- Whether capacity constraints are tightening or easing faster than consensus.
- Whether public supply proxies are strong enough to support a probability move.
- Which bottleneck matters most: wafers, packaging, memory, networking, power,
  or demand digestion.
- Whether a thesis is supported by official evidence, proxy evidence, or still
  only a research path.

## What It Does Not Try To Do

- Treat every news item as a thesis update.
- Move probabilities without an approved evidence baseline.
- Let proxy evidence silently replace direct evidence.
- Compress away domain distinctions between CoWoS, HBM, foundry capacity, and
  end-demand signals.

## Current Public Lesson

AI_CAP has become the strongest example of baseline maturity discipline. CoWoS
and HBM can both be important bottlenecks, but they may sit at different maturity
levels: one baseline may be ready to apply while another remains blocked by a
missing price or consensus proxy.

## Recent Public Status

The public portfolio currently treats AI_CAP as active. Its private engine has
adopted monthly compression and golden-case review patterns, but the public site
only exposes sanitized summaries.

## Next Human Review Focus

- Which bottleneck has the best recurring public proxy?
- Which thesis still depends on research-only evidence?
- Which local AI_CAP patterns are mature enough to promote to a broader
