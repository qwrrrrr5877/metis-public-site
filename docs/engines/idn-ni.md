# IDN_NI Engine

<p class="metis-section-label">ResourceCountry</p>

The IDN_NI engine studies Indonesia nickel policy, RKAB execution, substitution
flows, and how local policy implementation transmits into commodity and supply
chain outcomes.

## What It Tries To Judge

- Whether policy execution matches official policy direction.
- How permitting, quotas, and administrative timing affect supply availability.
- Whether substitution or displacement flows are becoming material.
- Which institution-path errors recur across resource-country policy cases.

## What It Does Not Try To Do

- Treat official announcements as execution facts.
- Collapse policy intent, administrative implementation, and market impact into
  one object.
- Assume every local constraint immediately becomes a global price signal.
- Publish raw source trails or unresolved policy predictions on the public site.

## Current Public Lesson

IDN_NI remains the seed example for resource-country policy transmission. Its
most useful public lesson is that institutional execution paths matter: a policy
can be directionally clear while the timing, enforcement, and market effect stay
uncertain.

## Recent Public Status

The public portfolio lists IDN_NI as active. Its current public role is to keep
resource-policy monitoring visible while private ledgers preserve the detailed
prediction and evidence state.
